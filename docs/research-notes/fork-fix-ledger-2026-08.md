### Status semantics (fix pass, 2026-08-14)

`not-a-gap` = **the feature exists in Hexwaste and our behaviour already matches the fork's
post-fix behaviour**. `not-applicable` = **the routine, field or call site does not exist here at
all** (including out-of-scope subsystems). Rows that had drifted between the two were corrected in
this pass: hunk 35 (`dudeIsAddicted(-1)`) and hunk 51 (`proto_critter_init` team) moved
`not-a-gap` → `not-applicable`; hunk 37 (lips) was re-justified and kept `not-a-gap` because our
lip-sync IS implemented and does yield the correct first phoneme.

**No-op C++ changes** (renames, retyping, formatting, comment-only edits — nothing that moves a
byte at runtime) are classified by whether the touched routine/call site exists in Hexwaste at all:
if it does, the no-op has nothing to differ from, so our behaviour trivially matches and the row is
`not-a-gap`; if the routine/call site is not ported here, the row is `not-applicable` regardless of
how trivial the C++-side change was. Applied consistently: #267 row 1/2 (pure `combat.cc`
renames — `ammoQuantity` → `roundsHitMainTarget` etc.) touches `attackCompute`, whose analogue
(`CombatEngine.cs:299`) is ported and unaffected by the rename, so it is `not-a-gap`, not
`not-applicable`. Commit `86787e2c9` (comment-only SSL-name corrections) touches
`interpreter_extra.cc`, whose opcode table (`ExternalArity.cs`) is also ported here and already
carries the correct names, so it too is `not-a-gap` — the two rows now rest on the same rule.

A terminal status may not rest on unread evidence. The #267 row was demoted from `not-a-gap` to a
split pair (a rename-only row plus a live-candidate row) once the `item.cc` `ammoSetQuantity`
clamp it depended on was actually read.

**Rule-consistency sweep (2026-08-14, second fix pass).** A review found the rule above was stated
generally but applied to only the two rows it names. Every other no-op-shaped row in the table was
re-checked against the pre-image at `e97087b`/the fork commit and its cited call site; nine rows
moved `not-applicable` → `not-a-gap` because both conditions held (confirmed no-op, confirmed
ported): `0e773d521` (weaponPerk→`Perk` retype, `PERK_INVALID == -1`), `20e80d4bb`

### Recurring judgment note

Several in-scope commits are the fork **repairing its own post-`e97087b` refactors** rather than
correcting a misreading of the original binary (`0f3023dc9`, `ab841916f`, `d9c24e1cc` — and the EDG
tile.cc series in full). The reliable test applied throughout: reconstruct the pre-image at
`e97087b` (`git -C reference/fallout2-ce show e97087b:src/<file>`) and confirm the "before" text
actually exists there. When it does not, the change is fork churn and cannot be a vanilla gap.
