(`.superpowers/sdd/fork-fix-shortlist.tsv`):

backends, and the fork's own EDG / sfall-extension features).

### Recurring judgment note

Several in-scope commits are the fork **repairing its own post-`e97087b` refactors** rather than
correcting a misreading of the original binary (`0f3023dc9`, `ab841916f`, `d9c24e1cc` — and the EDG
tile.cc series in full). The reliable test applied throughout: reconstruct the pre-image at
`e97087b` (`git -C reference/fallout2-ce show e97087b:src/<file>`) and confirm the "before" text
actually exists there. When it does not, the change is fork churn and cannot be a vanilla gap.
